About Catch and Release

Catch and release fishing is a conservation practice where anglers return fish back into the water after catching them. When done properly, it helps maintain healthy fish populations and protects important fisheries for future anglers. This project explains why catch and release is important and how anglers can safely release fish to improve survival rates.

Benefits of Catch and Release

Protects Fish Populations

Releasing fish allows them to continue growing and reproducing, helping maintain healthy populations.

Supports Conservation

Catch and release reduces overharvest and helps fisheries biologists manage fish populations sustainably.

Preserves Fishing Opportunities

Healthy fisheries provide better fishing experiences for future generations of anglers.

Protects Large Breeding Fish

Larger fish often produce more eggs and stronger offspring, making them important to release.

How to Properly Catch and Release Fish

  1. Use barbless hooks or circle hooks when possible.
  2. Land fish quickly to reduce exhaustion.
  3. Wet your hands before touching the fish to protect its slime coat.
  4. Keep the fish in the water as much as possible.
  5. Use rubber landing nets to reduce injury.
  6. Remove hooks carefully and quickly.
  7. Support the fish horizontally when handling it.
  8. Release the fish gently back into the water until it swims away strongly.
